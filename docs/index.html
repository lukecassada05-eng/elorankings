Methodology

Elo ratings, margin-adjusted

Standard Elo treats every win identically. We multiply the K-factor by a log-scaled margin of victory — a 30-point blowout earns more than a 1-point squeaker, but with diminishing returns to prevent rating inflation.

Each season's games are processed 10× for convergence. Teams with fewer than 4 games receive a rating penalty to suppress early-season noise. All ratings start at 1500 each season.

# expected win probability
EA = 1 / (1 + 10((RB−RA) / 400))

# margin-of-victory K adjustment
Kadj = K × ln(|margin| + 1)

# updated rating after game
RA′ = RA + Kadj × (SA − EA)

# iterated 10× · baseline 1500 · K varies by sport
